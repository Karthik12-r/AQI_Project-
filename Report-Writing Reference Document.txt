## 4. Abstract

This project analyzes winter air quality at the CPCB monitoring station Peenya, Bengaluru (site_163) across Winter 2023, Winter 2024, and Winter 2025, and evaluates a next-day AQI prediction experiment. The verified pollutant-derived daily AQI dataset (`peenya_aqi_2024_2025_validated_estimates.csv`) contains 731 calendar days (2024-01-01 to 2025-12-31), of which 668 have valid CPCB AQI values. An independent recalculation matched all 668 valid days within 0.0005 AQI units with 100% prominent-pollutant agreement. Winter descriptive analysis shows Winter 2023 recorded AQI mean 71.86 with high volatility (SD 49.64), Winter 2024 hybrid mean 83.52, and Winter 2025 pollutant-derived mean 82.72 with comparatively low volatility (SD 12.95). In 140 validated winter days, PM10 was the prominent pollutant on 117 days (83.57%). PM2.5 and PM10 correlated strongly (Pearson r = 0.881, Spearman rho = 0.889). A chronological next-day prediction experiment used 55 training examples (targets 2024-01-03 to 2024-02-29) and 74 held-out test examples (targets 2024-12-01 to 2025-02-28). No tested machine-learning model outperformed the persistence baseline on MAE/RMSE (persistence MAE 9.25, RMSE 11.92; Random Forest MAE 10.54, RMSE 13.49; Gradient Boosting MAE 12.41, RMSE 15.66; Linear Regression MAE 33.85, RMSE 46.71). Results are preliminary due to small samples and possible seasonal/domain shift. No following-year (post-2025) winter AQI forecast was performed.

**Objectives (from `reports/final_project_report.md` section 4):**
- Verify a CPCB-derived daily AQI dataset against independent hourly recalculation.
- Describe winter AQI patterns for Winter 2023, Winter 2024, and Winter 2025.
- Analyze pollutant dominance, correlations, PM2.5/PM10 ratios, and diurnal/monthly profiles.
- Build a date-aware next-day AQI prediction dataset with chronological train/test split.
- Compare persistence, linear regression, random forest, and gradient boosting on held-out winter 2025 data.
- Document limitations and avoid unsupported claims of model superiority or deployment readiness.

**Key findings (summary):**
- Verified daily dataset: 731 calendar days, 668 valid AQI; independent recalculation agrees within <=0.0005 AQI units; 100% prominent-pollutant agreement; 0 validity mismatches.
- Winter 2023: recorded hourly AQI averaged by day; mean 71.86, SD 49.64, median 53.96; 90/90 valid days; range 39.00-250.42; 6 Poor days.
- Winter 2024: hybrid (Dec 2023 recorded average + Jan-Feb 2024 pollutant-derived); 91 calendar days, 90 valid, 1 missing; mean 83.52, SD 21.89.
- Winter 2025: pollutant-derived; 90 calendar days, 81 valid, 9 missing; mean 82.72, SD 12.95; 81.1% Satisfactory days; no Poor/Severe days.
- Pollutant dominance (140 validated winter days): PM10 on 117 days (83.57%); O3 14 (10.00%); CO 7 (5.00%); PM2.5 2 (1.43%); 0 ties.
- PM2.5-PM10: Pearson r = 0.881, Spearman rho = 0.889 (N=139).
- PM2.5/PM10 ratio: combined mean 0.4981 +/- 0.0684 (median 0.5094).
- Next-day prediction: persistence baseline best (MAE 9.25, RMSE 11.92); no ML model outperformed it.

**Limitations (from workspace):**
- Small sample: 55 training and 74 test examples.
- Training (Jan-Feb 2024) and test (Dec 2024-Feb 2025) periods differ seasonally; possible domain shift.
- 16 of 74 test dates had at least one imputed predictor (17 instances).
- Winter 2023 uses recorded hourly AQI; later winters use pollutant-derived AQI; direct year-to-year equivalence not justified.
- Track 2 covers only 140 validated days and excludes incomplete-pollutant dates.
- No hyperparameter tuning or cross-validation beyond the specified chronological split.
- No claim of generalization to other locations, years, or operational deployment.

## 5. Introduction

### 5.1 Background

Winter air quality in Indian cities is frequently elevated due to shallow boundary layers, reduced dispersion, and continued emissions. Peenya, Bengaluru, is an industrial area with substantial traffic and manufacturing activity. The project uses CPCB monitoring data for the Peenya station (site_163).

### 5.2 Problem Statement

The project addresses two questions:
1. What are the observed winter AQI patterns and dominant pollutants at Peenya across recent winters?
2. Can next-day AQI be predicted from recent pollutant and AQI history using standard machine-learning models?

### 5.3 Aim

To analyze winter air quality at Peenya, Bengaluru using CPCB data and to evaluate a reproducible next-day AQI prediction pipeline.

### 5.4 Objectives

See Section 4 objectives list.

### 5.5 Scope and Study Area

- Station: Peenya, Bengaluru - CPCB (`site_163`), Karnataka, India.
- Winter 2023: 2022-12-01 to 2023-02-28 (90 calendar days).
- Winter 2024: 2023-12-01 to 2024-02-29 (91 calendar days, leap year).
- Winter 2025: 2024-12-01 to 2025-02-28 (90 calendar days).
- Pollutant verification and Track 2 cover 2024-01-01 to 2025-02-28 (150 calendar days in winter months; 140 validated).
- Step 5 prediction experiment: targets in 2024-01 to 2024-02 for training and 2024-12 to 2025-02 for testing.

### 5.6 Significance

The work emphasizes methodological transparency: distinguishing recorded hourly AQI from pollutant-derived daily AQI, preserving missing values, and avoiding leakage in model evaluation. It provides a verified, reproducible analysis and an honest evaluation of next-day AQI prediction, including a persistence baseline.

### 6.3 Data Cleaning and Missing-Data Handling

- Hourly rows parsed with `dayfirst=True`; metadata rows skipped.
- Duplicate `(date, hour)` rows collapsed by mean; out-of-range hour rows dropped.
- Daily aggregates: mean, min, max, std; `valid_hours` count; `data_completeness_pct = valid_hours/24*100`.
- Missing values preserved (not interpolated) in verified dataset.
- Next-day model: missing predictors imputed with training medians; imputation indicators created (`*_missing` columns). Missing target/lag rows excluded.
- Winter daily data completeness threshold not explicitly stated; descriptive stats use available days.

### 6.4 CPCB AQI Methodology

- CPCB AQI (Indian NAQI) computed as maximum of pollutant sub-indices (PM2.5, PM10, NO2, SO2, NH3, CO, O3) using CPCB breakpoint tables.
- Prominent pollutant = pollutant with the maximum sub-index.
- Daily AQI for pollutant-derived days = daily max sub-index (from daily mean concentrations, or as per code).
- Recorded AQI (Winter 2023) = daily average of CPCB hourly AQI values.
- Categories: Good (0-50), Satisfactory (51-100), Moderate (101-200), Poor (201-300), Very Poor (301-400), Severe (401-500).

## 10. Machine Learning Methodology (Step 5)

### 10.1 Dataset and Target

- Source: `data/processed/next_day_aqi_dataset.csv` (2,586 rows; 2024-01-01 to 2025-12-31).
- Task: predict next-day AQI (`target_AQI`) from current-day features.
- Features: PM2.5_t, PM10_t, NO2_t, CO_t, O3_t, AQI_t, AQI_t_minus_1, month, day_of_year.
- Rows with missing target or lag excluded.

### 10.2 Feature Engineering

- `AQI_t`: current-day AQI (feature date).
- `AQI_t_minus_1`: previous-day AQI.
- `month`, `day_of_year`: calendar features.
- Feature date = target date - 1 day; lag date = target date - 2 days.

### 10.3 Imputation

- Missing predictors imputed with training-set medians.
- Binary missingness indicators (`*_missing`) added.
- Medians: PM2.5_t 35.563; PM10_t 75.766; NO2_t 18.624; CO_t 0.831; O3_t 41.606; AQI_t 84.436; AQI_t_minus_1 83.07; month 1.0; day_of_year 30.0.
- Imputation instances: 17 across 16 of 74 test dates (mostly CO_conc, O3_conc, PM2.5/PM10/NO2).

### 10.4 Models and Hyperparameters (as in code)

- **Persistence baseline:** predicts AQI_t as next-day AQI.
- **Linear Regression:** scikit-learn LinearRegression (default).
- **Random Forest:** RandomForestRegressor(n_estimators=300, min_samples_leaf=2, random_state=42, n_jobs=-1). Note: `final_project_report.md` mentions max_depth=8, min_samples_leaf=3; code does NOT set max_depth and uses min_samples_leaf=2. Report this discrepancy.
- **Gradient Boosting:** GradientBoostingRegressor(n_estimators=200, learning_rate=0.05, max_depth=2, random_state=42). Note: report mentions subsample=0.8; code does not set subsample (default 1.0). Report discrepancy.

### 10.5 Train/Test Split (chronological)

- Train: targets 2024-01-03 to 2024-02-29; 55 examples; feature dates 2024-01-02 to 2024-02-28.
- Test: targets 2024-12-01 to 2025-02-28; 74 examples; feature dates 2024-11-30 to 2025-02-27.
- Split is by date, not random, to avoid leakage.
